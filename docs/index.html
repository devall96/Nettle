What's in it

Playback

  • Never transcodes. Nettle plays the file itself, so your server's CPU stays idle and the picture is untouched.
  • HDR10, HDR10+, HLG and Dolby Vision, including Dolby Vision profile 5.
  • Audio passthrough: Dolby Digital, Atmos, TrueHD, DTS-HD and DTS:X go to your receiver as they are.
  • Format badges you can trust: 4K, Dolby Vision or Atmos only show when that is what's really playing.
  • Skip intros, recaps and credits, with an Up Next countdown into the next episode.
  • Scrubbing with thumbnail previews, and chapter marks on the seek bar.

Live TV

  • A cable-style guide for everything NostalgiaTV has scheduled, with a live "now" line.
  • Original-quality channels: each program plays from its real file on your server, at the live position.
  • Surf like a TV: channel up and down, type a channel number, or jump back to the last channel.
  • A tuning screen with the channel's logo covers each switch.

Browsing

  • Home with a spotlight carousel, Continue Watching, Next Up and Recently Added, on screen the moment Nettle opens.
  • Libraries with sorting, filters (unwatched, genre, decade, 4K / HD / SD) and an A–Z strip.
  • Details with cast and crew, trailers, extras, More Like This and a version picker.
  • Search across your library by kind.
  • Requests with Seerr: browse what's trending, then request a movie or the seasons you want.

Together

  • Watch together (Jellyfin SyncPlay): play, pause and seek happen for everyone in the group at the same moment.
  • Play on Nettle from other Jellyfin apps: your phone can start, queue, pause and seek.
  • Multiple profiles with a "Who's watching?" picker and Quick Connect sign-in.
  • Up to date everywhere: resume points and watched status stay in sync with your server.

On Android TV

  • Built for the remote, not a port of the desktop window, with the same look and features.
  • The TV's own hardware decoders (Media3): HDR and Dolby Vision reach your TV as they are.
  • Refresh rate follows the video, so films play at 24 Hz without judder.
  • Fast on cheap boxes: about 0.8 seconds from a cold start to Home on a 2 GB onn 4K box.

Look and privacy

  • Sixteen themes, from true black for OLED screens to three light ones. This page wears them too.
  • Keyboard and remote ready: arrow keys move focus the way a D-pad does.
  • No telemetry of any kind.
  • Passwords are never stored. Only Jellyfin's session token is kept, encrypted on your device.
  • Updates itself from Settings → Updates on all three platforms.
